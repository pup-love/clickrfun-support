Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Little answers to common Clickrfun questions.

What is Clickrfun?

Clickrfun is a social corner of the internet with profiles, public chat rooms, private messages, virtual pets, badges, treats, a shop, and games.

How do I make an account?

Visit Clickrfun and open Login, then choose the sign-up option and follow the prompts. If email confirmation is requested, check your inbox for the sign-up link.

How do I chat with other members?

Sign in to Clickrfun and choose a public room, or use Private DMs to message another member when the feature is available to your account.

What are treats?

Treats are virtual rewards used around Clickrfun. Check My Treats and the Shop to see what's available.

How do badges and stickers work?

Explore Clickrfun and try the available activities to unlock collectibles. Your collection shows the badges and stickers you've earned.

How do virtual pets work?

Visit My Virtual Puppy or My Virtual Kitty to meet and care for your companion. Check their needs and try the available pet actions.

Can I use Clickrfun on my phone?

Clickrfun is designed to fit different screen sizes. If something looks odd, refresh the page or try an up-to-date browser.

How can I keep my account safe?

Never share your password or private login codes with anyone, including support. Only enter login details on the official Clickrfun website.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Try refreshing Clickrfun. If the issue continues, note what you clicked and what happened, then use the live chat below to contact support—even if Clickrfun itself won't load.
